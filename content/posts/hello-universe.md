## The equation that started it

Everything in particle physics bookkeeping goes back to the relativistic energy–momentum relation:

$$E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2$$

Add two particles' four-momenta, and the *invariant mass* $M$ of the pair doesn't care which frame you're in. That single idea is how the Z boson shows up at 91 GeV and the Higgs at 125 GeV.
